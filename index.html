GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I book an appointment?+

Open Book Appointment, choose a service, select an available stylist and date, then check the time slots.

Can I change or cancel a booking?+

Use the booking-management link provided with your appointment, or contact the salon for assistance.

Where can staff sign in?+

Use the Staff login link at the top of this page to open the existing SalonDiva sign-in screen.

Can I create an account?+

Yes. Select Create account to open the existing SalonDiva registration page. Account permissions are managed by the application.
